    Limit and stop orders stay open until filled or cancelled. While Folio is running during market hours (9:30 AM–4:00 PM ET on trading days), an order fills on the first live trade that crosses its price, at that trade's price. It's also checked at least once a minute. Cash and shares aren't reserved, so an order is rejected if you no longer have enough when it triggers.
